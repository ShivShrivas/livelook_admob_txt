Privacy Policy

App: Livelook: A GPS Camera App
Last Updated: October 27, 2026

1. Information Collection & Usage

Livelook: A GPS Camera App operates on an entirely client-side model. We do not harvest, store, or transmit your media, coordinates, or identity attributes to remote servers.

A. Location Services (GPS & Network)

The app accesses device location solely to fetch real-time geographic metadata (latitude, longitude, altitude, and address) to imprint them onto photos and videos captured inside the app. This information stays on your device and is never uploaded or shared.

B. Camera & Microphone

Camera and microphone permissions are utilized strictly to capture photo and video files with audio. We do not access camera feeds outside active user-triggered recording sessions.

C. Local Device Storage (DCIM)

Captured files are written directly to your device's DCIM media directory so you can access them in your gallery. We do not inspect, upload, or modify existing files outside the app's scope.

2. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on essential Google platform components to render map interfaces or core device services:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Maps Platform

3. Data Retention and Control

Because all data remains on your physical hardware, you have full authority to delete individual photos, recordings, or wipe app cache at any time via your device settings or gallery.
